Bundled 2K maps: Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Earth day map, Earth clouds,
Earth night map, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.
Original download filenames start with `2k_`; local files omit that prefix.
The JPEG pixel data is unchanged. GravityExplorer applies artistic illumination,
cloud transparency, atmospheric glow and night-side masking at render time.

Solar System Scope describes these maps as derived from NASA and other data,
with artistic color adjustments and reconstructed details where data is missing.
These are illustrative planetary maps, not current observations or precision
cartography. Sizes, rotation, lighting and distances in the exhibit are artistic.

The original procedural fallback artwork and generated Saturn rings are part of
GravityExplorer. Website reference designs were studied for composition only;
no Behance, Pinterest or Dribbble artwork was copied or redistributed.
